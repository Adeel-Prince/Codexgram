# Legal and commercial decisions to confirm

The repository does not determine the following. Each bracketed item remains open in the draft Terms of Service. Confirm the answer before publishing; do not publish the draft with placeholders.

1. **Contracting party:** What exact legal name, entity type, and address operates Codexgram? Is the contracting party an individual or company? `[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]` and `[NOTICE ADDRESS]` are placeholders.
2. **Eligibility and age:** What minimum age applies, and will the service be offered to minors or only adults? The app has no age check. Choose an age policy and implement any required gate; do not treat a contractual sentence alone as a technical check.
3. **Geographic availability:** Which countries/regions can sign up? The governing law and venue must be chosen with the operator’s place of business and target markets in mind.
4. **Commercial model:** Is access intended to be free? No payment implementation was found. Are any paid plans planned? If so, list the exact price, billing interval, taxes, auto-renewal/cancellation mechanics, trial terms, and who processes payment before enabling them.
5. **Refunds:** What refund/cancellation policy will apply to any future purchase, and how will it interact with mandatory consumer rights and app-store purchase rules? No refund flow or purchase mechanism is present in the code.
6. **Dispute resolution:** Do you want court litigation only, arbitration, or another process? The draft leaves governing law and court venue bracketed and does not add an arbitration/class-action waiver.
7. **Warranty, liability, and indemnity:** Confirm the intended warranty disclaimer and liability cap with counsel. Fill `[LIABILITY CAP / BASIS]` and jurisdiction-specific carve-outs; decide whether the user indemnity clause is acceptable for the operator’s markets.
8. **Contact and legal notices:** Provide a monitored support/legal email and postal notice address. No working support contact was found in the repository. Confirm how users will receive material terms changes.
9. **Privacy policy and data retention:** Supply the actual privacy policy, retention periods, rights-request process, and backup/log deletion position. Deleting an account triggers batched cleanup in the current code, but no backup/log schedule is stated there.
10. **Content ownership and service license:** Confirm that users keep ownership and approve the narrow license in the draft (hosting, storing, displaying to service users, and processing through the service vendors). Decide how long that license should survive after content/account deletion and whether any promotional/publicity use is intended. The code does not decide this.
11. **Moderation and enforcement:** Can the operator actually review/remove content, restrict an account, and process abuse/IP notices? The repository has no working report, blocking, moderation, or admin suspension feature; related settings entries are placeholders. Terms should be revised only to match capabilities and a staffed process that really exist.
12. **Demo content and third-party terms:** Confirm whether the Unsplash-backed demo seeding path is enabled in any live deployment and whether its image licenses and attribution requirements are satisfied. Confirm the operational service providers and applicable privacy/terms disclosures for Clerk and Convex.
13. **Acceptance flow:** Add a clear, affirmative acceptance step that links to the current Terms and Privacy Policy before account creation, and retain the accepted version/time if required. The current sign-up form has no such control.

## Working assumptions in the draft

- Codexgram currently has no paid feature or subscription, based on the repository; this is not confirmation that no external or planned charge exists.
- User posts, profile fields, and comments remain the user’s content; Codexgram receives only a service-delivery license. This is a proposed contract position, not a fact encoded in the app.
- No arbitration or class-action waiver is included pending the dispute-resolution decision.
- No AI terms are included because no AI feature or AI provider is present in the inspected application code.
- No operator-side suspension or content-removal promise is included because no such mechanism is evidenced in the current repository.
