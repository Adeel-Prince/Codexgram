A little help.

Quick answers for your account and the things you can share.

01

How do I update my profile?

Open your profile, go to Settings, then choose Edit profile. You can change your username, display name, bio, website, location, and profile photo.

02

What can I upload?

Photo posts can be up to 10 MB. Videos can be up to 50 MB and 60 seconds. Photo stories can be up to 10 MB.

03

Where did my story go?

Stories appear in the active story view for 24 hours. After that, they no longer appear there.

04

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings and choose Delete Account. The request starts background removal of your account and associated posts, stories, comments, likes, and follows. Cleanup may take time to finish.
